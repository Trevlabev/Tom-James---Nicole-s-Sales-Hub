Receiving sequence

  1. Open and retain packing lists.
  2. Match client, SP, order/COF, item, and quantity.
  3. Separate new inventory, completed alterations, returns, and office material.
  4. Inspect condition and completeness.
  5. Check in when required.
  6. Assign a physical location and next action.

Stop and escalate

  • Wrong client or unknown garment
  • Missing or extra piece
  • Visible damage
  • Record has no strong identifier
  • Physical item has no matching Trello/Excel record
  • Record exists but item cannot be found

Security boundary

A portal is not an authentication system.

This static build contains no login, server-side permissions, encrypted database, secret storage, or secure client-record system.

Safe for the portal

  • Navigation and program descriptions
  • Generic training scenarios
  • Public-safe checklists and templates
  • Links to properly permissioned systems
  • Embeds that enforce their own authentication

Keep out of public Pages

  • Client names, addresses, measurements, or orders
  • Internal contacts and personal phone numbers
  • Credentials, tokens, or API keys
  • Payroll, banking, deposit, or payment records
  • Unapproved internal manuals and screenshots
Noindex is not security. The included robots and meta directives discourage indexing, but anyone who can reach a public Pages URL may still view the content. Use access-controlled hosting for confidential material.

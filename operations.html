Daily operations

Run the work in the order risk appears.

This page is the replacement's operating map: open the day, receive and identify garments, investigate exceptions, complete client movements, then reconcile and hand off.

01

Open the day

  • Review today and tomorrow on the calendar
  • Check overdue and due-soon Trello cards
  • Sort Excel Critical, High, then Review
  • Review rush, Watch List, alterations, shipments, and aging inventory
  • Prepare Nicole's decision list
Planner placeholder
02

Receive and control

  • Open boxes and preserve packing lists
  • Match client, SP, garment, order, and COF
  • Separate new inventory, alterations, and returns
  • Inspect and assign the physical location
  • Update Trello and Excel
Receipt tool placeholder
03

Investigate exceptions

  • Check WIP milestones and Watch List items
  • Contact factories and providers with exact identifiers
  • Compare ETCs to client-required dates
  • Record source, result, and next follow-up
  • Escalate with a recommendation
WIP/Watch placeholder
04

Close and hand off

  • Reconcile changed Trello and Excel records
  • Update every physical garment movement
  • Pack and stage tomorrow's garments
  • List Critical risks and waiting items
  • State the exact decisions Nicole must make

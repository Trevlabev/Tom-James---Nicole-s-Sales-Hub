5-Step Daily Operating Model

How the Garment & Order Move Through the Office

Every tool connects to the same local browser workspace so intake becomes tracking, tracking becomes a handoff, and finished garments leave the active queue cleanly.

Step 1

Triage

Review calendar fittings, critical alterations, Watch List delays, and rush orders.

Step 2

Receive

Check in incoming shipments, tag garments, and log physical rack storage locations.

Step 3

Investigate

Follow up with tailors (Gegi, Naziha) and factories on stalled WIP orders and ETC dates.

Step 4

Deliver

Prepare client delivery, schedule fitting appointments, and generate shipping labels.

Step 5

Reconcile

Update local records, stage tomorrow's rack, and copy the Daily Handoff Brief.

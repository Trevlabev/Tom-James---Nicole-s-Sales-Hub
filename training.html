Replacement training

Train for judgment, not memorization.

The website supports a ten-day progression from office orientation to independent triage, with alterations receiving the deepest practice.

Day 1 — Role, security, and physical office

Receiving, racks, alteration intake, factory return, shipping, and staging locations.

Day 2 — Secure Site, Contact Control, WIP, and Watch

Find client, order, COF, garment, stage, due date, and exceptions.

Day 3 — Trello, Excel, and reconciliation

Create complete records and resolve stale or duplicate entries.

Day 4 — Shipments and inventory

Match packing lists, inspect garments, check in, locate, and document.

Days 5–7 — Alterations intensive

Intake, photographs, local routing, factory returns, ETCs, reported completion, physical verification, and escalation.

Day 8 — Completed alterations and measurements

Client-return planning and Nicole-approved measurement updates.

Day 9 — Calendar, deliveries, shipping, and handoff

Integrate physical garments with the calendar and next-day preparation.

Day 10 — Independent practical assessment

Run opening review, process representative work, explain decisions, and complete sign-off.

Scenario lab

Use fictional cases to practice provider delays, missing garments, unclear photos, partial returns, and measurement decisions.
